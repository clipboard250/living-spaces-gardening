The "Get verified" button only appears when you're signed into wraykiamesha@gmail.com — that's the account the business is registered under. Any other account and you won't see it.

2

2a In the Google Maps search bar, type Living Spaces Gardening and tap it.

3

3a On the "Select a way to get verified" screen, tap Submit a Business Video.
3b Tap Next.
3c Read the "Tips for getting started" screen. Don't tap Start Recording yet — finish reading Steps 4 and 5 first.
3d When Google asks for camera, microphone, and location access, tap Allow for all three. Location must be on — Google uses it to confirm you're at the right address.
4

What Google needs to see

Google requires all three of these in one continuous recording:

  • Location — the house number 2621 or the street sign on 4th Ave S
  • The business is real — the Living Spaces Gardening logo on the truck, clearly readable, and you unlocking it with the key
  • You're authorized — tools inside the truck, plus anything printed with "Living Spaces Gardening" on it (invoice, work order, permit)
Rules — don't skip these

One continuous take. No stopping, no cuts. No faces in the frame. No talking. Good daylight. Hold the phone steady. Location services must be on.

5

Record the video — exact sequence

Have your truck keys ready and one document with "Living Spaces Gardening" on it. Go outside. Tap Start Recording and follow this sequence without stopping.

One continuous take — about 90 seconds

0:00–0:15Point the camera at the house number 2621 or the street sign. Hold steady for 5 seconds.
0:15–0:30Walk toward the truck while filming.
0:30–0:50Hold the camera on the Living Spaces Gardening logo on the side of the truck. Don't move until the name is clearly readable.
0:50–1:05Use the key to unlock the truck. Open the door. Keep filming — no cuts.
1:05–1:25Show tools inside — shovels, pruners, plant flats, irrigation parts, whatever's loaded.
1:25–1:40Hold up the document with "Living Spaces Gardening" on it. Cover any client info with your thumb. Hold for 5 seconds.
6

6a After recording, watch it back. Make sure the house number or street sign is visible, the logo is readable, and the document text is legible.
6b If anything's off, tap Try Again. Re-record as many times as needed.
6c When it looks right, tap Upload Video.
