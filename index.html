Why Native

Your yard should work for you, not the other way around.

You're out there every weekend watering, mowing, replacing plants that keep dying. It shouldn't feel like a second job.

Native Florida plants are built for this climate. They handle heat, salt, storms, and drought. They bring in butterflies and birds without constant intervention.

We design landscapes built for Florida, not against it. Less work for you. More life in your yard.

About Us

Kiamesha Wray

Kiamesha Wray is a certified Florida Master Naturalist and one of the first FANN credentialed professionals in the state.

She walks your property and immediately starts mapping what belongs where, what will thrive in your soil, what will draw pollinators, what will anchor the space near your entry.

Living Spaces Gardening has been serving Tampa Bay since 2018. Kiamesha and her husband Justis design landscapes that look beautiful and function as living ecosystems. Native plants sourced from trusted Florida growers. Hardscaping that feels intentional and permanent. Gardens that mature, not deteriorate.
