The Living Spaces Difference

Stop fighting your own yard.
Work with it.

Florida's climate is intense. Most landscaping fights it—with irrigation systems that run daily, plants that die every summer, and designs that look great in a catalog but struggle in your actual soil.

We do the opposite. We look at what your yard actually is—the sun, the drainage, the soil type—and design something that belongs there. Native plants that Florida grew without your help for ten thousand years.

Meet Kiamesha

The woman who knows what
your yard wants to be.

Kiamesha Wray founded Living Spaces Gardening in 2018 after years watching beautiful, capable Florida yards get beaten into submission by generic landscaping that fought the climate instead of working with it.

She's a Florida Master Naturalist (2014) and among the first FANN-credentialed professionals in the state—credentials that mean she doesn't just know plants, she understands ecosystems. Featured in Homes & Gardens magazine (November 2025).
