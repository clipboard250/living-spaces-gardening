Why It Matters

Stop fighting
your own yard.

If you've been replacing dead sod every summer, watching your plants struggle through the heat, or just feeling like your yard never quite looks right — it's not you. It's the plants.

Florida has its own rules. The salt, the floods, the brutal summer sun — most landscaping just isn't built for it. Native plants are. They've been doing this for thousands of years without any help.

We design yards that are beautiful the way Florida is beautiful — wild and alive and effortless once they get going. A yard that attracts butterflies. That survives a storm. That you actually enjoy spending time in.

Let's Talk About Your Yard

Salt air, heavy rains, August heat waves, flooding — native plants handle all of it without you lifting a finger.

Meet the Team

She knows this land.

Kiamesha Wray has been a Florida Master Naturalist since 2014. She grew up learning the ecology of this state — her mother was an archaeologist who studied the Tocobaga tribe, the people who called this land home long before anyone paved it over. That history shapes the way Kiamesha sees every yard she walks into.

She's one of the first FANN (Florida Association of Native Nurseries) credentialed professionals in the state. When she looks at your yard, she's not just thinking about what looks good. She's thinking about what belongs there, what will thrive, what your piece of Florida could look like if it got to be itself again.

Living Spaces Gardening has been doing this work since 2018, alongside Kiamesha's team — Justis, Osiris, and Ether — who bring the same care and craft to every project.
