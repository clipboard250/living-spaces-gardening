Gardens

Your garden should work for you, not the other way around.

You're out there every weekend watering, mowing, replacing plants that keep dying. It shouldn't feel like a second job.

Native Florida plants are built for this climate. They handle heat, salt, storms, and drought. They bring in butterflies and birds without constant intervention.

We design landscapes built for Florida, not against it. Less work for you more natural living for your garden.

Questions homeowners ask
about native landscaping

Intentional, not wild. Native yards use plants that belong here — firebush, muhly grass, gumbo limbo, coontie — arranged around your soil, sun, and drainage. They look designed and feel alive.

Yes — once established. Florida's dry season runs October through May. Native plants are built for it. Non-native turf isn't, which is why it keeps dying and costing money to replace.

Planting for looks without considering the climate. Beautiful plants keep dying because they were never meant to grow here. Native plants work with Florida's heat, salt, and drought instead of against them.

Meet the family

The Wray-Weisensel Family

Kiamesha Wray and Justus Weisensel are the couple behind Living Spaces Gardening and Living Restorations. Family owned, family operated.

Kiamesha is a certified Florida Master Naturalist. She grew up on dig sites with her archaeologist mother and spent weekends camping along Florida's rivers and springs with her father. Justus is a premier woodworker and builder. He's the one grading your soil, placing the boulders, building the things that have to last.

Their kids fuel everything they do. Every native garden they restore is a piece of the Florida the next generation will inherit. Their crew has been with them for years. When they take on your project, these are the people who show up.
