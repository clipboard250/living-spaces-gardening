Gardens

Your garden should work for you, not the other way around.

You're out there every weekend watering, mowing, replacing plants that keep dying. It shouldn't feel like a second job.

Native Florida plants are built for this climate. They handle heat, salt, storms, and drought. They bring in butterflies and birds without constant intervention.

We design landscapes built for Florida, not against it. Less work for you more natural living for your garden.

How we help your property

Whether it's a full redesign or simply getting the right plants in the ground, we design around what Florida gives you.

01

Florida Native Landscape Design

A full garden design built around plants that belong in Florida. We match every element to your soil, sun, and water so it thrives without constant maintenance.

Text about this →
02

Hardscape Design

Patios, pathways, dry river beds, retaining walls—built to work with your landscape—not against it. Functional stonework that feels like it was always there.

Text about this →
03

Irrigation & Lighting

Florida's dry season (yes we have one of those) runs eight months—October through May. That's most of the year with under three inches of rain per month. We design irrigation that puts water exactly where your plants need it, so nothing dies waiting for June.

Text about this →
04

Permaculture Design

A garden that feeds you back. Edible gardens and food forests designed to produce year-round while rebuilding the soil underneath them. Grow what belongs here—and eat it too.

Text about this →
05

Specimen Tree Sourcing and Planting

Mature native trees placed for immediate impact—structure, shade, and presence from day one. No waiting ten years. Every tree sourced from trusted Florida growers who know what survives here.

Text about this →
06

Garden Maintenance & Monitoring

Your landscape changes with every season, even in Florida. We stay with it—pruning, adjusting—and watching for problems before they spread. Most gardens decline after install. Ours get better every year.

Meet us

Kiamesha Wray

Kiamesha Wray is a certified Florida Master Naturalist and one of the first FANN credentialed professionals in the state.

She walks your property and immediately starts mapping what belongs where, what will thrive in your soil, what will draw pollinators, what will anchor the space near your entry.

Living Spaces Gardening has been serving Tampa Bay since 2018. Kiamesha and her husband Justis design landscapes that look beautiful and function as living ecosystems. Native plants sourced from trusted Florida growers. Hardscaping that feels intentional and permanent. Gardens that mature, not deteriorate.
