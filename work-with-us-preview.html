Why this crew

Real work. Real plants. Real ecology.

Most landscaping jobs are mowing and blowing. This is not that. We design and install Florida native gardens, plant specimen trees, build hardscapes, and restore habitat. The work is harder, the learning curve is steeper, and the gardens stay alive long after the install.

01

Learn the plants

Work alongside Kiamesha Wray, a Florida Master Naturalist credentialed since 2014 and one of the first FANN credentialed professionals in the state. Native plant knowledge that takes years to build, taught on the job.

Real projects, real variety

Habitat gardens, dry river beds, specimen tree installs, hardscape pathways, permaculture beds, rain gardens. No two days look the same. Featured in Homes and Gardens magazine for coastal resilience work.

The honest version

Who this is actually for.

Better to figure this out before we both spend time on it. Here is what the work really looks like.

This is for you if

  • You like being outside more than being inside
  • You actually care about Florida ecology and native plants
  • You want to learn the plants, not just push them in the ground
  • You show up when you say you will
  • You are physically capable of digging, lifting, hauling, and working in Florida heat
  • You take pride in clean lines, healthy plants, and finished work

This is not for you if

  • You are looking for indoor or sit-down work
  • You want lawn-and-mow work that does not change
  • You do not want to learn plant names
  • Florida summer heat is a dealbreaker
  • Showing up on time is optional for you
  • You see landscaping as a stepping stone, not the work

Good fits

The kinds of people we love to meet.

We are always open to meeting good people. When the right project comes up, we reach back out. These are the roles we tend to grow into.

Hands on

Crew members

People who love being outside, working with their hands. Planting, mulching, hardscape building, soil grading. We teach the plant knowledge. You bring the work ethic and curiosity.

Experienced

Lead installers

Experienced landscapers who can run a job site. Reading designs, directing crew, quality control on installs. Native plant experience preferred but trainable.

Plant nerds

Native plant designers

People who already know Florida natives and want to design with them. Landscape architecture or horticulture background a plus. FANN credential too.
