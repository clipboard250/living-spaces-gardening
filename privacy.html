Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

Living Spaces Gardening ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website livingspacesgardening.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site or contact us.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us via text message, phone call, email, or our website contact form. This may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, and details about your landscaping project.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, provide estimates, and deliver our landscaping and garden design services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text Messaging

When you tap one of our "Text Us" buttons, your device's native messaging app opens with a pre-written message to our business number (727-710-7400). We do not automatically send messages or subscribe you to any list. You are always in control of what you send.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use basic analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Vercel. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. These services may collect anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable measures to protect any personal information you share with us, though no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. Contact us at kiamesha@livingspacesgardening.com or call 727-710-7400.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
